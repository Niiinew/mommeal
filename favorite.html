Chicken Stir-Fried Vegetables
30 Min - Easy
soy
Diced chicken breast (300g)
Mixed vegetables (200g)
Soy sauce (2 tbsp)
Garlic (3 cloves)
Vegetable oil (1 tbsp)
1
Heat oil in a large pan or wok over medium-high heat.
2
Add minced garlic and stir-fry for 30 seconds until fragrant.
3
Add diced chicken and cook for 5-7 minutes until golden brown.
4
Add mixed vegetables and stir-fry for 3-4 minutes.
5
Pour soy sauce and mix well. Cook for another 2 minutes.
6
Serve hot with steamed rice. Enjoy!
